# Third-party protocol research

The Oukitel local protocol implementation is informed by the public protocol research and testable implementation in [bordeux/ha-oukitel-powerstation](https://github.com/bordeux/ha-oukitel-powerstation), Copyright (c) 2026 Chris Bednarczyk, MIT license. The complete license notice is retained in `licenses/oukitel-research-MIT.txt`.

VOLTOS uses an independently implemented TypeScript packet parser and an iOS Network.framework transport; it does not require Home Assistant. No vendor app secrets or user device auth keys are embedded in the app.
